Purpose
This tool can be used to experiment with and manipulate sounds, audio files, or even entire songs in creative ways. It opens up a lot of possibilities for changing how audio behaves depending on different situations or environments. One of the first experiments I tried was making the music feel like it naturally changed depending on the area I was in. For example, as I moved between different locations, I would make each audio track would smoothly fade in or out to match the environment, creating the feeling of actual dynamic audio. A lobby area could have one style of music, a shop could have another, and an arcade a third. Instead of having a single song play everywhere, I'd make the audio adapt to my location, making each area feel more... independent.
