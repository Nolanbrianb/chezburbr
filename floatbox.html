FLOATBOX

a floaty little sandbox  ·  v1.3.0

WASD move · Space jump · Shift sprint · Q brakes
Ctrl crouch — sprint+Ctrl = slide · midair Ctrl = dive · release & re-press near ground = roll
LMB summon · RMB hold to grab & fling · E hold in hand
R rotate gizmo · G freeze · X delete · F catalog · T throw item · 1-9 / scroll hotbar · Esc menu
Cars: E enter/exit · WASD drive · Q handbrake · C doors/trunk · V camera.
Aim down sights with RMB while holding a gun · platforms: E then aim & E again to set the path.
Weapons: sword LMB swing · bomb E light then LMB throw · mortar LMB lobs onto your crosshair · nuke & turret E on the object.
Holding something? X drop · LMB fire/throw · R reload · E use.
Sneak + double-click a cube to recolour. Ladders are climbable.
